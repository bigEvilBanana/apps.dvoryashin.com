What TennisSum is

TennisSum is a tennis score keeper for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. You enter each point for singles or doubles; the app calculates games, sets and tiebreaks. You can also enter a result after a match and correct a saved result.

Access

Access requires a verified Lifetime purchase or an active subscription, including an activated eligible trial or confirmed billing grace period. Apple displays the price and confirms purchases. A pending or cancelled purchase does not provide access.

Subscriptions and purchases

  • Monthly is an auto-renewing subscription. Eligible customers may get a free trial; its length and the price after it are shown in the app and in the App Store before you confirm.
  • Subscriptions renew through Apple unless you cancel in Apple subscription settings at least 24 hours before renewal. Cancelled auto-renewal does not remove an already paid access period.
  • Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no trial or renewal. Buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel an existing subscription.
  • Purchases are made through Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under App Store rules.

Results, levels and statistics

TennisSum records the points you enter; it does not detect the winner of a rally. Its levels and statistics describe recorded activity, not tennis skill.
