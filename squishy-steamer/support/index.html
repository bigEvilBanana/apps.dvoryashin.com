What is free?

Making dumplings is free and unlimited: the free doughs, fillings, faces and toppings, the steamer, the basket, guests, the sticker album and photo cards. Every five album stickers add a new free item to the trays.

How does Unlock Everything work?

It is a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no coins. It unlocks every item with a lock in the trays (special doughs, fillings, faces and toppings) and the album cards that need them. The price is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm.

I asked a parent to approve the purchase.

With Ask to Buy, the purchase screen shows "Request sent" and you can keep playing. Once the request is approved, everything unlocks by itself.

I already bought it. How do I get it back?

Open Settings (the gear) and tap Restore purchase, or tap Restore purchase on the Unlock Everything screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where are my dumplings saved?

On your device only. There is no account, so they are not backed up to us. Deleting the app removes them. Settings → Clear all dumplings empties the basket and the shelf; your album stickers and your purchase stay.

There's no sound.

Sounds follow the silent switch on the side of the phone. You can also turn sound effects, music, the name announcer and vibration on or off in Settings.
