Privacy Policy

DIY Dumpling · Last updated October 14, 2026

DIY Dumpling does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game works offline.

Your dumplings stay on your device

The dumplings you make, your basket, your sticker album, guest stickers and your settings (sound, music, vibration, name announcer) are stored only on your device. We never see, receive or transmit them. Deleting the app removes them.

In-app purchase

Unlock Everything is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The app asks Apple only whether the purchase is active so it can unlock items and restore them. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Photo cards and sharing

A photo card is a picture the app draws of your dumpling. It leaves the app only when you tap Share and choose where to send it in Apple's share sheet. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks your permission to add the picture to your photo library; the app never reads your photos.

Voice

Dumpling names are spoken by the speech voice built into iOS, on your device. Nothing you say or hear is recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.
