Privacy

Dopamine Detox · 28 October 2026

Dopamine Detox works without creating an account. It has no advertising SDK, and your app-use history is not sent to the developer or any service. Purchase statistics are described under Purchases.

Information on your iPhone

Your onboarding answers, method settings, selected apps, local activity records, session history and purchase-access records are stored on your device. The app and its Screen Time extensions share the information needed to apply your chosen restrictions.

Screen Time selections use tokens provided by Apple. The app uses Screen Time permission to monitor its selected activities and apply restrictions. It records events from its own methods; it does not claim to measure all phone use or time saved. A missing observation is kept as unknown.

Purchases

Apple processes payments, purchase approval, refunds and subscription management. Dopamine Detox uses StoreKit to verify purchase status and keeps a local access record so the app and its extensions can check access. Access is decided on your device from Apple’s verified purchase information.

To count purchases, trials, renewals and refunds, the app sends purchase information to RevenueCat, a subscription service that processes it for the developer: the product, price and currency, transaction and renewal details, App Store country and a random app user ID created on your device. RevenueCat also receives Apple’s purchase notifications for this app and the technical details needed for the connection, such as app and iOS version and IP address. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, Screen Time selections or activity history, and it does not decide whether you have access. Payment card details are not available to the developer or RevenueCat. RevenueCat privacy policy.

Links and support

When you open a support, privacy, terms or research link, the destination website handles that visit under its own privacy practices. Opening a link does not send your saved app selections or history.

If you choose to email support, the developer receives what you include in the message and uses it to answer your request. Do not include your Apple Account password or payment card information.

Your choices and local data

You can turn off the saved main method, end a session, or change Screen Time permission in iOS. Turning off restrictions does not erase your saved settings and history. Ending paid access also keeps those records available in the app.

Removing the app may remove local records. Device backups may contain local data and may restore it later. Restore purchases checks purchase access and sends the restored purchases to RevenueCat for the statistics above; it does not retrieve deleted history.
