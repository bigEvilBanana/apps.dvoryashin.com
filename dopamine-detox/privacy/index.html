Privacy

Dopamine Detox · 21 October 2026

Dopamine Detox works without creating an account. It has no advertising SDK, developer analytics SDK or developer server for your app-use history.

Information on your iPhone

Your onboarding answers, method settings, selected apps, local activity records, session history and purchase-access records are stored on your device. The app and its Screen Time extensions share the information needed to apply your chosen restrictions.

Screen Time selections use tokens provided by Apple. The app uses Screen Time permission to monitor its selected activities and apply restrictions. It records events from its own methods; it does not claim to measure all phone use or time saved. A missing observation is kept as unknown.

Purchases

Apple processes payments, purchase approval, refunds and subscription management. Dopamine Detox uses StoreKit to verify purchase status and keeps a local access record so the app and its extensions can check access. Payment card details are not available to the developer, and purchase information is not sent to a developer server.

Links and support

When you open a support, privacy, terms or research link, the destination website handles that visit under its own privacy practices. Opening a link does not send your saved app selections or history.

If you choose to email support, the developer receives what you include in the message and uses it to answer your request. Do not include your Apple Account password or payment card information.

Your choices and local data

You can turn off the saved main method, end a session, or change Screen Time permission in iOS. Turning off restrictions does not erase your saved settings and history. Ending paid access also keeps those records available in the app.

Removing the app may remove local records. Device backups may contain local data and may restore it later. Restore purchases checks purchase access; it does not retrieve deleted history from a developer server.
