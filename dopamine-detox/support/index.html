Dopamine Detox · 21 October 2026

Getting started

Choose a method in the app. After your purchase is confirmed, allow Screen Time access, choose the apps to include, and save. A purchase alone does not turn on restrictions. You do not need to create a Shortcuts command or automation.

Pause and Daily budget are alternative main methods. A session can run alongside either method and takes priority for its selected apps. Changing a draft does not change the saved method until you confirm it.

Check purchase access

Open Methods → Settings → Check access. If you purchased before, use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for that purchase. An unavailable price or a failed check does not by itself mean that paid access has ended.

A pending purchase may need Apple approval. If the app says a purchase was found but access could not be saved, retry that check instead of starting another purchase. Your chosen setup is kept.

Manage Monthly or Lifetime

Open Methods → Settings → Manage subscription to review a known Monthly subscription with Apple. Turning off automatic renewal keeps access through the confirmed paid period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Buying Lifetime does not cancel an existing Monthly subscription.

Check restrictions

Open the saved method from Today or Methods and check its status. If Screen Time permission is missing, follow the app’s access prompt. Pause requires individual apps; its selection does not include categories or websites.

To stop the main method, use its turn-off confirmation in the app. To stop a session, open the session and confirm ending it. These recovery actions remain available without a new purchase. Dopamine Detox only manages its own restrictions; other Screen Time settings may still apply.

If paid access has ended, the app attempts to remove its restrictions. If it cannot confirm removal, Settings shows a retry action. A session interrupted by lost access does not count as completed.

History

History contains the app’s recorded events. Unknown usage is not zero, and an interrupted session is not a completed session. Restore purchases does not restore deleted local history.

Contact

Email support@dvoryashin.com with your app version, iOS version and a short description of what happened. You may include a screenshot after checking it for personal information. Never send passwords or payment card details.
