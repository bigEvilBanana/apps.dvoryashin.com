Support

PupDuty

Need help? Email support@dvoryashin.com. In the app, Settings → Support diagnostics → Contact support opens an email with a privacy-safe report already attached; that helps us answer faster.

Is PupDuty free?

You can log ten entries on your iPhone for free, without an account. Sharing the journal with your care team needs a subscription. New subscribers get a free trial; the length and price are shown in the app before you confirm.

How do I invite my partner, family or dog walker?

Care team tab → Invite your care team. They get an email link, sign in with Apple and see the same journal. Up to four people join for free; one person pays.

Why does "Next potty break" say 0 of 6?

PupDuty starts estimating after six pees and six poops. The bars fill with every entry. The estimate is based only on your own entries; it is an estimate, not a promise.

Can I fix an entry?

Tap the entry on Home or in History to change its time or delete it. Entries that are still being shared with your care team can be deleted but not re-timed until they have synced.

I subscribed on one phone but the other phone says free.

Sign in with the same Apple account, then tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Settings → Manage subscription inside PupDuty. Your journal stays readable after the subscription ends.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete account removes your account and, if you are the Owner, the care team. See the privacy policy for what is stored.
