Support

PupDuty

Need help? Email support@dvoryashin.com. In the app, Settings → Support diagnostics → Contact support opens an email with a privacy-safe report already attached; that helps us answer faster.

Is PupDuty free?

You can log ten entries on your iPhone for free, without an account. Continuing to log and sharing your journal need an Owner's Annual or Lifetime plan. Both cover one care team with unlimited puppies and up to four invited Members. Annual may include a free trial if you are eligible; the offer and price are shown before purchase. Lifetime is a one-time purchase without a trial.

Sign in with Apple or an email link before choosing a plan, purchasing or restoring. Signing in alone does not enable paid sharing. Invited Members sign in and accept their invitation without buying a plan.

How do I invite my partner, family or dog walker?

Care team tab → Invite your care team. They get an invitation email, sign in with Apple or an email link, and accept the invitation to see the care team's journals. Up to four people join for free; the Owner pays.

Why does "Next potty break" say 0 of 6?

PupDuty starts estimating after six pees and six poops. The bars fill with every entry. The estimate is based only on your own entries; it is an estimate, not a promise.

Can I fix an entry?

Tap the entry on Home or in History to change its time or delete it. Entries that are still being shared with your care team can be deleted but not re-timed until they have synced.

I bought a plan on one phone but the other phone says free.

Sign in to the same PupDuty account. If your purchase is missing, tap Restore purchases on the purchase screen or in Settings, using the Apple Account used for that purchase. Restore works for Annual and Lifetime.

How do I cancel?

As a signed-in Owner, open subscription management in PupDuty Settings to use Customer Center. You can also cancel Annual in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Your journal stays readable after paid access ends. Lifetime has no renewal to cancel.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete account removes your account and, if you are the Owner, the care team. A Member deleting their account does not delete the care team's shared journal. Account deletion does not cancel Annual or reset trial eligibility, and restoring a purchase does not recover deleted journal data. See the privacy policy for what is stored.
