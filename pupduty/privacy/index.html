Privacy Policy

PupDuty · Last updated 2 September 2026

PupDuty is a shared puppy journal for the people who look after one puppy together. This page explains what the app stores, where it goes, and how you delete it. Short version: your entries are about your puppy, not about you, and we never sell or share them for advertising.

Using PupDuty without an account

You can start logging without signing in. Everything you enter then (your name, your puppy's name, birth date, photo and up to ten entries) is stored only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us until you sign in and subscribe. Deleting the app deletes that data.

What we store when you sign in

DataWhy
Your Apple account identifier and the email address you share through Sign in with Apple (which may be Apple's private relay address) To create your PupDuty account, sign you in again, and send sign-in and invitation emails.
The display name you choose, and the names of people you invite So every entry shows who logged it.
Your puppy's name, optional birth date and optional photo To show the puppy in the journal. The photo is private to your care team; it is never public.
Journal entries: type (pee, poop, accident, meal, water, sleep, wake, no potty), time and who logged them This is the journal itself and the basis of the next potty break estimate.
Subscription status (active, ended, refunded) and the product type To know that the care team's Owner has an active subscription. We never see card details.
A push notification token and your device's time zone and language Only if you turn on reminders or care team alerts.
Limited product facts (for example that an entry was logged, an invitation was accepted, or a subscription started), without names, puppy names, entry content or device identifiers To understand whether PupDuty works and improve it. This is first-party and closed-schema; there is no advertising or third-party analytics SDK in the app.

Who sees your care team's data

Only the people in your care team. The Owner invites people by email and can remove them at any time. We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising, and we never use your entries or photos to train models.

Service providers

  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and push notifications.
  • RevenueCat processes subscription status for us. It receives your App Store receipt and your PupDuty account identifier, never your name or email.
  • Resend delivers sign-in and invitation emails to the addresses you provide.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH hosts our servers and database in the European Union.

Each provider only receives what it needs for its job and acts on our instructions.

How long we keep data

As long as your care team exists. When you delete your account or the care team from Settings, the data becomes unavailable immediately and is removed from our systems; encrypted backups may retain it for up to 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage that in your Apple account settings.

Your choices

  • Export your journal as CSV or PDF at any time (Owner).
  • Leave a care team, or as Owner remove people or delete the care team.
  • Delete your account in Settings.
  • Turn reminders and alerts off in Settings or in iOS notification settings.
  • Email us to access, correct or delete your data.

Children

PupDuty is for adults who look after a puppy. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not medical or training advice

The next potty break estimate is calculated from your own entries. It is an estimate, not a promise, and it is not veterinary, medical or training advice.
