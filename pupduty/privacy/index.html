Privacy Policy

PupDuty · Last updated 7 September 2026

PupDuty is a shared journal for the people who look after one or more puppies together. This page explains what the app stores, where it goes, and how you delete it. Entries include who logged them. We never sell or share your entries for advertising.

Using PupDuty without an account

You can start logging without signing in. Everything you enter then (your name, your puppy's name, birth date, photo and up to ten entries) is stored only on your iPhone. Signing in sends account information to our service. Local entries are added to your own care team only after sign-in and confirmation of paid access; they are not added to a care team you join by invitation. Deleting the app deletes the local data.

RevenueCat may exchange purchase and service data before you sign in, as described below. PupDuty does not send first-run activity, your local profile or your local journal as analytics. You must sign in before viewing a purchase screen, purchasing or restoring purchases.

Who sees your care team's data

Only the people in your care team. The Owner invites people by email and can remove them at any time. We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising, and we never use your entries or photos to train models.

Service providers

  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and push notifications.
  • RevenueCat provides purchase screens, purchase management and purchase analytics, and helps verify Annual and Lifetime access. Before sign-in, it may process an SDK-generated or previously saved user identifier, purchase history and receipt-derived purchase status, App Store region, locale, and app, device and SDK information needed for its service. After sign-in, we send your random PupDuty account identifier, linking purchase information to your account. We do not send RevenueCat your name, email, care-team data, puppy or journal content, or advertising identifiers.
  • Resend delivers sign-in and invitation emails to the addresses you provide.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH hosts our servers and database in the European Union.

How long we keep data

We keep care-team data while the care team exists. If you are the Owner, deleting your account or care team in Settings removes that care team. Its active records and photos become unavailable immediately; encrypted backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days. If you are a Member, leaving or deleting your account does not delete the care team's shared journal; the local shared data is removed from your device.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage that in your Apple account settings. Creating a new account does not recover a deleted care team or reset App Store trial eligibility. Restoring a purchase does not restore deleted journal data.

Your choices

  • Export your journal as CSV or PDF at any time (Owner).
  • Leave a care team, or as Owner remove people or delete the care team.
  • Delete your account in Settings.
  • Turn reminders and alerts off in Settings or in iOS notification settings.
  • Email us to access, correct or delete your data.

Children

PupDuty is for adults who look after a puppy. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not medical or training advice

The next potty break estimate is calculated from your own entries. It is an estimate, not a promise, and it is not veterinary, medical or training advice.
