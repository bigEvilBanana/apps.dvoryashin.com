What PupDuty is

PupDuty is a shared journal for the people who look after one puppy. You log potty breaks, accidents, meals, water, sleep and wake times, see who logged what and when, and get an estimate of the next potty break based on your own entries.

Free use

You can log up to ten entries on your iPhone without an account or a subscription. Those entries stay on your iPhone and remain readable if you decide not to subscribe.

Subscriptions and purchases

  • Annual renews automatically for the same period at the price shown unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period.
  • Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no renewal. Annual and Lifetime unlock the same features.
  • Purchases are made through Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under App Store rules.
  • One subscription covers one care team: the Owner pays, up to four invited people join for free.

Your care team

The Owner decides who joins and can remove people or delete the care team. Everyone in a care team sees the same journal, puppy profile and names. Invite only people you trust with that information. If the Owner's subscription ends, the journal stays readable and exportable, and new entries wait until it is active again.
