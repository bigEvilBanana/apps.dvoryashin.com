ZeroPouch: Quit Nicotine Pouch

ZeroPouch helps you quit or cut down on nicotine pouches — tracking money saved, nicotine avoided, and your day-by-day progress. Here are answers to common questions.

How do I get started?

On first launch, pick "cold turkey" or "taper down," and enter your usual habit (strength, pouches per day, price per can). After setup, choose monthly or lifetime access. Once the purchase or eligible trial is active, the tracker opens with your numbers; you can change the setup later in Settings.

What's free vs. Pro?

After setup, full tracking access requires either a monthly subscription or the one-time lifetime purchase. Both options unlock the same tracker, taper plan, reminders, progress insights, recovery timeline, and widget. Safety SOS, local data export, local data deletion, purchase restoration, and viewing existing history remain available without an active purchase.

How does the monthly trial work?

Apple determines trial eligibility for each account. If the paywall says you are eligible, the monthly option is free for seven days and then renews monthly unless cancelled. If eligibility cannot be confirmed, ZeroPouch does not promise a trial. The App Store purchase sheet always shows the terms before confirmation.

I used an earlier free version. Is my history gone?

No. Your existing local history stays on your device. Version 1.1 explains the new access model once; without an active purchase you can still view, export, or delete that history and use Safety SOS, but new tracking entries, reminders, and widget content remain locked.

I bought Pro on another device / reinstalled.

Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or read-only screen. Monthly and lifetime purchases are tied to your Apple Account. Previous lifetime buyers keep the same product ID and do not need to buy again.

Can I fix a day I logged wrong?

Yes. On the Progress tab, tap any day under "Recent days" and adjust the count. Your streak and totals recalculate — fixing a miscount never punishes your progress.

Is my data backed up or synced?

No. Everything is stored privately on your device only (no account, no cloud). A new device starts fresh. This keeps your data fully private.

Is this medical advice?

No. ZeroPouch is for general information and motivation only and is not medical advice. For free US support, call 1-800-QUIT-NOW or visit smokefree.gov.
