Privacy Policy

ZeroPouch: Quit Nicotine Pouch · Last updated September 2026

Your quit and taper data stays on your device.

What we collect

ZeroPouch has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, or cross-app tracking. The app does not transmit your quit date, pouch counts, cravings, notes, taper plan, or other health-related entries.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter — your quit date, pouch counts, cravings, notes, taper plan, and settings — is stored only on your device. We never see it, transmit it, or have access to it. You can erase this local data at any time from Settings → Delete my data. Deleting the app also removes its local data, but does not erase purchase records retained by Apple or RevenueCat.

Purchases

Monthly and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. ZeroPouch uses RevenueCat to validate receipts and determine whether access is active. RevenueCat receives an app-specific anonymous identifier and purchase history for app functionality and purchase analytics. ZeroPouch does not send RevenueCat your name, email, payment details, advertising identifier, or any quit, craving, taper, or health-related data. Purchase information is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Health & age

ZeroPouch is for general information and motivation only and is not medical advice. For free US support, call 1-800-QUIT-NOW or visit smokefree.gov.

Children

The app does not transmit locally entered quit or health-related data from anyone, including children. Purchase processing uses the limited anonymous purchase information described above.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
