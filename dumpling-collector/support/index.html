Frequently asked

Are the values real prices?

They're point-in-time community estimates based on resale ranges, shown for reference only. They are not live quotes, offers, or appraisals, and real prices move constantly.

Where is my collection saved?

Your owned checklist, duplicate counts, wishlist, notes, and photos stay on your device. The app has no account, so that collection data isn't backed up to us. Deleting the app removes its local data. The app connects to Apple and RevenueCat only for purchase and access verification and purchase analytics.

How does Collector Pro work?

Collector Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase. There is no trial and no subscription. The purchase screen shows the current App Store price before you confirm. New users need Collector Pro to open the collection tools. If you already have a saved collection, you can keep viewing it; adding or changing items requires Collector Pro.

I already purchased access. What should I do?

Choose Restore Purchases on the access screen. People who originally bought the app before it became a free download also keep full access. If access still does not appear, email us.

A dumpling I have isn't in the catalog.

The catalog is a June 2026 snapshot and grows with updates. Email us the name and we'll consider it for the next catalog update.

Is this an official app?

No. It's an unofficial fan-made tracker, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by RMS USA, Crazy Fun, Five Below, or any toy maker.
