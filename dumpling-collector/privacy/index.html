Privacy Policy

Dumpling Squishy Collector · Last updated September 12, 2026

Dumpling Squishy Collector stores your collection on your device. Purchase and access information is processed through Apple and RevenueCat so the app can sell and restore its one-time lifetime unlock and understand purchase activity.

Purchase information

The app uses Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat to process purchases, restore prior purchases, and determine whether lifetime access is active. RevenueCat processes purchase history, product and transaction information, and an app user identifier for access verification and purchase analytics. These analytics help us understand purchases and revenue. No app account or sign-in is required.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in the app — the dumplings you mark as owned, duplicate counts, your wishlist, notes, and any photos you attach — is stored only on your device. We never see it, transmit it, or have access to it. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it.

Photos

If you choose to attach a photo to an item, the app reads only the single image you select and stores it locally inside the app. It does not access your wider photo library, location data, or any other information.

Service providers

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase-related information under their own privacy terms. RevenueCat is used for purchase functionality and purchase analytics. Read RevenueCat's privacy policy for more information.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
