Privacy Policy

Padel Score Tracker: PadelSum · Last updated 3 October 2026

Your match history stays on your devices.

What we collect

PadelSum does not require an account and has no advertising, tracking or developer analytics service. The app does not transmit your match scores, settings, onboarding answers or Health data to the developer.

Your data stays on your device

Match scores, settings and onboarding answers are stored on your device. Your paired iPhone and Apple Watch exchange match data and deletion records through Apple's WatchConnectivity. iPad history is local to that iPad. Device backups may contain this local data.

Apple Health on Apple Watch

When you choose Record workout on Apple Watch, PadelSum requests Health permission, reads heart rate and active energy during that workout, and saves the workout to Apple Health. Choosing Score only requires no Health access. Health data is not sent to the developer or included in diagnostic reports. You can change Health permissions in system settings.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and subscription management through the App Store. PadelSum verifies Apple transactions and stores a local access copy. Purchase receipts and tokens are not sent to a developer server or included in diagnostic reports.

Sharing and diagnostic reports

Sharing a result or diagnostic report is optional. Preview the diagnostic report before using the system share sheet; you choose the recipient. A support email contains only what you choose to send. The developer uses that message to answer your request.

Deleting results

Delete a result to remove it from visible history and update statistics and progress. A local deletion marker is retained to prevent the result returning through paired sync. Removing the app may remove local history; restoring a purchase does not restore deleted matches.

Children

The app does not transmit locally stored match, settings or Health data from anyone, including children.
